# How to Edit the Spreadsheet Download

## Overview
The downloaded pricelist spreadsheet is a professional Excel file (.xlsx) designed for tracking product availability and status. You can edit the **Status**, **Qty**, and **Notes** columns to track your inventory.

## What You Can Edit

### Status Column (Column E)
- **Click on any cell** in the "Status" column to see a dropdown menu
- Choose from three options:
  - **Unavailable** (highlighted in red) = Not available/Not a focus
  - **Purchased** (highlighted in green) = Focus SKU's already in system
  - **Recommended** (highlighted in yellow) = Not currently listed, but would like approval

### Quantity Column (Column F)
- Enter the quantity you have on hand
- Use this to track stock levels

### Notes Column (Column G)
- Add any additional comments or details about the product
- Use this for order notes, special instructions, or observations

## What You Cannot Edit
The following columns are **locked for protection**:
- Product name
- SKU
- Format
- Price
- Column headers and brand sections

## Saving Your Changes
1. Make your edits (Status, Qty, Notes)
2. **Save the file** using Ctrl+S (Windows) or Cmd+S (Mac)
3. Keep the file with a clear naming convention like: `Pricelist_[Company]_[Date].xlsx`

## Conditional Formatting
The spreadsheet uses **automatic color highlighting** based on your Status selections:
- Rows with "Unavailable" → Red background
- Rows with "Purchased" → Green background
- Rows with "Recommended" → Yellow background

This makes it easy to see your selections at a glance.

## Sharing & Collaboration
- You can email the edited spreadsheet to team members
- Each person can work on their own copy
- Combine results manually or contact support for bulk updates

## Compatible Programs
- ✅ **Microsoft Excel** (full support)
- ✅ **Google Sheets** (import the .xlsx file)
- ⚠️ **Apple Numbers** (conditional formatting may display differently)

## Tips
- Use the **Status dropdown** before entering Qty and Notes
- The legend at the bottom of the sheet explains all status colors
- Keep backups of edited files for your records
- Download a fresh pricelist after uploading new CSV data to ensure you have the latest products
